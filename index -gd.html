Brands

Designed layouts, visual elements, palettes and typography for digital brands that focus on video games and web3 ecosystems.

New, dark version of the token page vs bright version, inspired by the old TOY designs


TOY & TAIX: A web3 ecosystem supporting TAIX- an AI streamer assistant. Modern, sleek visuals following the design trends set by Apple. The simple backdrops are supported with bright pastel coloured shapes, in places distorted by glassy elements








Qorpo World: A merging between a luxurious finance app and video games. Clear, direct design lets the brands video game characters stand out. This is the old style of the brand, please see my web design page here if you wish to see new visual identity I designed, incorporated into a web UI.








Aneemate: Colourful and whimsical, a call for adventure. A video game for Pokémon fans. I wanted to give more space to the characters and have the designs serve only as a slight highlight to their own designs. The game features a set of elements- I attributed two shades of pastel color to each of these, supporting them with simple visual elements resembling stylized paint strokes.
