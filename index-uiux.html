AneeMate

In-game UI/UX || Marketplace integration

Aneemate is a creature-catching adventure game with WEB3 integrations, featuring multiplayer PvP and co-op mode. I co-created the visual identity of this project. My role in it's UI/UX was to come up with intuitive ways to navigate the in-game menu, with all it's features- the game HUD, the in-game inventory and coming up with ways to seamlessly integrate it's blockchain aspects- such as minting assets at the end of each game round. Aris Arcanum

UI/UX || Character Art

A roguelite where the player has an ability to combine various spells to create their own, unique attacks. The game also features a balanced set of de/buffs to experiment with, a varied selection of bosses and enemies and even a few NPCs to chat with along the way. I had the opportunity of designing the user interface, a challenging task as many of the features required mulitple iterations and playtestings. The final reception was positive, the systems proved intuitive enough to not break the flow and enhance their exerience in the mgical library.

Check out Aris on Steam

Tamitos App

Visual Identity || UI/UX

Mobile app where people generate points via sport activities, which are then converted to real value currency throuhg sponsors. The app features a throuhg donation system inspied by the most popular fundraiser apps, with a simple sports and social media system. Join a community, create streaks and get digital medals to share to your own social media. The sports tracking systems are provided throuhg well-known sporting apps such as Strava or Apple Health.

Project is currently close to being fully developed.

Thus Sang the Trees

UI/UX || Tutorialisation ||Level Design

A mix of gardening sim and horror highlighting the importance of cooperation with nature. Project of multiple masters students where I acted as a vision holder and team leader.
Try the game!

The unusual gameplay promoting cooperative themes with a non-human agent required a lot of iteration in tutorialisation. The end product combines subtle cues in the level design with pop ups to optimize the learning curve and achieve the designed experience.
