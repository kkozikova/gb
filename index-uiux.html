AneeMate

In-game UI/UX || Marketplace integration

Aneemate is a creature-catching adventure game with WEB3 integrations, featuring multiplayer PvP and co-op mode. I co-created the visual identity of this project. My role in it's UI/UX was to come up with intuitive ways to navigate the in-game menu, with all it's features- the game HUD, the in-game inventory and coming up with ways to seamlessly integrate it's blockchain aspects- such as minting assets at the end of each game round. Thus Sang the Trees

UI/UX || Tutorialisation ||Level Design

A mix of gardening sim and horror highlighting the importance of cooperation with nature. Project of multiple masters students where I acted as a vision holder and team leader.

itch.io

The unusual gameplay promoting cooperative themes with a non-human agent required a lot of iteration in tutorialisation. The end product combines subtle cues in the level design with pop ups to optimize the learning curve and achieve the designed experience.
