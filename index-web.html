TAIX AI

AI Streamer and streaming assistant.

Created visual identity for an intelligent streaming AI, co-devloped with AWS innovation labs. Worked on interfaces taking note of the set Twitch framework ,which would seamlessly incorporate the TAIX plugin, both directly on Twitch and on the TAIX AI website. TAIX's design is calm, friendly and inviting, made to be as simple as can be. It's aim is to make the WEB3 space more accessible to the general public.
